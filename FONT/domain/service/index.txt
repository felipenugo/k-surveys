Domain services: Provide business logic and validations for domain entities.

Files:
- UserService.java: Handles user-related business rules (login, register, edit profile).
- SurveyService.java: Business rules for creating and managing surveys.
- QuestionService.java: Business rules for adding and validating questions.
- AnswerService.java: Business logic for handling different answer types.
- ResponseService.java: Handles creation and validation of responses.
